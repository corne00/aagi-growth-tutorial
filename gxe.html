Where do G, E and G × E appear?

Environmental sequences generate different growth conditions. Genotypes differ in their responses, so genotype contrasts can change across environments and during the season.

\[M_{ge}(t)=\mu(t)+G_g(t)+E_e(t)+I_{ge}(t).\]

At each common day, we subtract genotype and environment means to obtain the double-centred interaction. These are descriptive orthogonal sums-of-squares fractions, not REML variance-component estimates. No APSIM maturity information is used to align predictions.

Show R code: G/E/G×E decomposition
M <- biomass_matrix
mu <- mean(M)
G <- rowMeans(M) - mu
E <- colMeans(M) - mu
I <- sweep(sweep(M, 1, G + mu, "-"), 2, E, "-")
fraction <- c(mean(G^2), mean(E^2), mean(I^2))
fraction <- fraction / sum(fraction)
