The APSIM wheat dataset

We use APSIM-wheat simulations from Bustos-Korts and colleagues: 199 genotypes, four Australian locations and 31 years. Biomass is available daily.

APSIM simulates crop growth from physiological parameters, weather, soil and management. Our reduced model uses biomass observations and weather-derived predictors.

Other crop traits

We do not use markers, APSIM soil-water status, leaf area or other simulated crop states as predictors. Thermal time and trailing rainfall are calculated from weather.
