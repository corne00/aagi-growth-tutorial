Approximating the response

We approximate the response using candidate functions of the environmental drivers. Splines describe curved responses; product terms allow interactions. The response is nonlinear in its drivers and linear in its coefficients.

\[f(\mathbf u)=\sum_{k=1}^{K}\beta_k\phi_k(\mathbf u).\]

The compact library contains 16 features: an intercept, three natural-spline functions each for temperature, thermal time, radiation and 14-day rainfall, a day-length term, and two thermal-time interactions.
