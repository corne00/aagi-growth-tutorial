Interpreting environmental responses

Weather drivers are correlated. Accumulated local effects (ALE) summarise local changes in the fitted growth-rate response within the observed driver distribution.

\[\delta_k=\frac{1}{n_k}\sum_{i:\,u_{ij}\in I_k}\left[f(z_k,\mathbf u_{i,-j})-f(z_{k-1},\mathbf u_{i,-j})\right].\]

We accumulate the local differences and centre the curve using empirical bin weights. The reference sample consists of 6,000 evenly indexed environment-days, each weighted equally. One-dimensional ALE does not display every interaction and is not a causal effect.
