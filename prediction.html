Predicting biomass in held-out years

We evaluate each posterior response along a held-out weather sequence and integrate it to predict biomass. The genotype and location have been observed in other years.

\[B^{(s)}_{ge}(t)=B_{ge}(0)+\int_0^t f^{(s)}_g(\mathbf u_e(v))\,dv.\]

Held-out performance

Entire year blocks are withheld. The basis, knots and scaling are learned from training years only. This tests transfer across years at known locations and genotypes; it does not test new genotypes or new locations.
