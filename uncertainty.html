What does an uncertainty interval describe?

Credible intervals describe uncertainty in the fitted biomass trajectory. Predictive intervals additionally include the interval discrepancy specified by our likelihood.

The simulator data contain no added measurement noise. Residual variance represents reduced-model discrepancy. Predictive discrepancies are independent between fitted intervals and accumulate along each path; credible intervals omit those discrepancies.

Coverage must be assessed, not assumed. Narrow intervals can miss APSIM trajectories when the response model is incomplete.
