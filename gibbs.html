Bayesian fitting with Gibbs sampling

Gibbs sampling alternates between coefficients, shrinkage precisions and residual variance. Every retained joint draw defines a response function.

\[\mathbf y\mid\boldsymbol\theta,\sigma^2\sim N(\mathbf Z\boldsymbol\theta,\sigma^2I),\quad\theta_k\mid\lambda_{q(k)}\sim N(0,\lambda_{q(k)}^{-1}).\]

Coefficients and genotype deviations are sampled jointly using block Gaussian algebra. ARD continuously shrinks coefficients; its precision is not an inclusion probability.

Priors and conditional equations
\[\lambda_{0k}\sim\operatorname{Gamma}(2,1),\quad\lambda_{Gk}\sim\operatorname{Gamma}(2,0.02),\quad\sigma^2\sim\operatorname{InvGamma}(2,0.1).\]

Gamma parameters are shape and rate; inverse-Gamma parameters are shape and scale. Coefficient priors are independent of residual variance. All coefficients use the documented integrated-feature scaling.

\[V=(\mathbf Z^\top\mathbf Z/\sigma^2+\Lambda)^{-1},\quad m=V\mathbf Z^\top\mathbf y/\sigma^2,\quad\boldsymbol\theta\mid\cdots\sim N(m,V).\]
