Why model growth dynamically?

Final biomass does not describe when growth occurred or how growth responded to the environment. Different growth trajectories can reach similar endpoints.

Repeated measurements let us study a response function relating environmental conditions to the rate of biomass accumulation.

\[\frac{dB_g(t)}{dt}=f_g(\mathbf u(t)),\qquad B_g(t)=B_g(0)+\int_0^t f_g(\mathbf u(s))\,ds.\]
