Reducing correlated features

Correlated basis functions can produce a wide, redundant design. Singular value decomposition replaces the nonconstant feature family with fewer coordinates, estimated from training data.

\[X=U\Sigma V^\top,\qquad\widetilde X=XV_r.\]

The fitted extension retains 99% of training spectral energy and is evaluated on the first held-out year block. The prior acts on the reduced coordinates, so this is a distinct model variant. Feature compression does not imply low-rank genetic effects.
